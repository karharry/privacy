Privacy Policy

Applies to the Android apps published by karharry (TechLounge). Last updated: 23 November 2026.

Summary

These apps do not collect, store on any server, sell or share your personal data. They contain no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking libraries, and they do not require an account.

Data the apps handle

Anything an app saves (settings, progress, files you choose) stays on your device. The "Per-app details" section below lists the permissions each app requests and why. If an app is not listed there, it follows the summary above.

Network access

Unless an app's section says otherwise, the apps work offline and make no network connections of their own. Where an app does connect to a service, its section names the service and what is sent.

Google Play

If you install an app through Google Play, Google handles the download and may process install and crash data under Google's Privacy Policy. The developer does not receive personal information from this.

Children

The apps do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Open source

The apps are open source under the MIT License, so you can inspect exactly what they do in their GitHub repositories at github.com/karharry. The MIT License covers the source code; this policy covers your data.

Changes
